About Me

I am a Research Scientist at Bosch, working on autonomous driving intelligence. My long-term goal is to advance autonomous driving from modular pipelines toward unified, foundation-model-driven systems that are robust, adaptable, and deployable in safety-critical environments. I am open to research collaborations in autonomous driving, VLM, and VLA that advance next-generation intelligent systems.
Our team at the Bosch always has internship openings for research on VLA+AD and related topics. Feel free to reach out!
